## Overview

Fractal Entropic Geometrodynamics (FEG) proposes that spacetime is not a continuous manifold but a discrete causal set whose geometry, matter content, and gauge forces emerge from two axioms, two theorems, and two laws:

| Component | Name | Domain |
|---|---|---|
| Axiom 1 | The Causal Set Postulate | Spacetime is a discrete partial order generated by Poisson sprinkling |
| Axiom 2 | The Non-Commutative Postulate | Quantum phases are evaluated over a von Neumann algebra |
| Theorem | Chentsov-Petz | The metric is uniquely the (quantum) Fisher Information Metric |
| Theorem | Kuratowski | Planarity of the UV graph forbids $K_5$ and $K_{3,3}$, forcing $K_4$ crystallisation |
| Macroscopic Law | Law of Large Numbers | Statistical convergence governs the continuum limit ($\delta \sim 1/\sqrt{N}$) |
| Microscopic Law | Sole Principle of Interaction | All forces arise from preservation of unitarity across discrete causal links |

Gravity emerges as the thermodynamic equation of state of the causal ensemble (Jacobson's Theorem). The strong force emerges as a topological obstruction: $K_4$ causal simplices (Skyrmion knots, $B=1$) crystallise at the Kuratowski UV limit, and their $S_3$ permutation symmetry must be gauged to $SU(3)$ to preserve unitarity under continuous quantum phase evolution (Petz's Theorem).

## The Simulation

The `simplex_simmulation/` directory contains a Rust implementation of the Kuratowski Calculus that demonstrates the emergence of baryonic matter as a topological necessity. The simulation operates on pure integer logic and discrete combinatorial topology. Floating-point arithmetic enters only at the final macroscopic averaging step.

The simulation generates a Poisson-sprinkled causal set ($N = 50{,}000$ events), applies Kuratowski vertex contractions to crystallise $K_4$ topological defects, and measures the spectral dimension $d_S(t)$ via random walk return probability for both the bare vacuum and the defect-modified graph.

Key results:

- **Vacuum**: $d_S$ diverges toward $\approx 15.79$ (boundary saturation)
- **Baryonic anchor** ($K_4$ defect): $d_S$ stabilises at $\approx 8.36$ (topological retention)
- **Transitional coupling**: $\alpha_{\text{trans}} \approx 0.31$, consistent with the observed strong coupling at the tau mass scale

See [`simplex_simmulation/README.md`](simplex_simmulation/README.md) for build instructions, dependencies, and troubleshooting.

## Pedagogic Material: Modulo Synthesis

The `pedagogic_booklets/` directory contains the Modulo Synthesis pedagogic project -- a comprehensive attempt to teach discrete physics from first principles.

### Volume I: The Geometry of Spacetime (Macroscopic)

Establishes the foundations of the macroscopic regime. Begins with Axiom 1 (the Causal Set postulate) and the Law of Large Numbers, then derives:

- The Fisher Information Metric as the unique spacetime metric (Chentsov's Theorem)
- The Benincasa-Dowker action as the exact discrete replacement for the D'Alembertian
- Einstein's equations as the thermodynamic equation of state of causal horizons (Jacobson's Theorem)
- The cosmological constant as Poisson noise ($\Lambda \sim 1/\sqrt{N}$)
- Spectral dimension flow from $d_S = 2$ (UV) to $d_S = 4$ (IR)

### Volume II: The Geometry of Matter (Microscopic)

Descends to the microscopic regime. Begins with Axiom 2 (the Non-Commutative postulate) and the Sole Principle of Interaction, then derives:

- $K_4$ causal simplices as Skyrmion knots forced by the Kuratowski UV limit
- The Petz metric as the quantum extension of the Fisher metric on von Neumann algebras
- $SU(3)$ gauge symmetry from the $S_3$ permutation skeleton of the $K_4$ simplex
- Color confinement as a topological obstruction against non-planar graph transitions
- Charge quantisation from the 3-fold simplex geometry

### Emma's Thought Experiments

A companion booklet of intuitive scenarios designed to make discrete physics accessible to a general audience. Each experiment connects a familiar physical phenomenon to the rigorous formalism of Volumes I and II:

- The Falling Elevator (Equivalence Principle via Jacobson and Chentsov)
- Counting the Sand (Dark Energy via the Law of Large Numbers)
- The Giant's Whisper (Hierarchy Problem via Kuratowski's Theorem)
- The Secret Tunnel (Entanglement via causal non-locality)
- The Quantum Ghost (Wave-particle duality via path integrals on the causal set)

## Quick Start

### Run the simulation

```bash
cd simplex_simmulation
cargo build --release
cargo run --release -- 50000 10
```

### Compile the booklets

Requires XeLaTeX (TeX Live). Run twice for TOC resolution.

```bash
cd pedagogic_booklets
xelatex modulo_synthesis_vol_I.tex && xelatex modulo_synthesis_vol_I.tex
xelatex modulo_synthesis_vol_II.tex && xelatex modulo_synthesis_vol_II.tex
xelatex emma_thought_experiments.tex && xelatex emma_thought_experiments.tex
```
